Your DAW's audio interface and ASIO settings are not changed.
DawAudioStreamer sends a streaming copy of your DAW's audio to OBS and Discord
while your DAW plays normally.

----------------

OBS
---

1. In OBS, click [+] under Sources.
2. Add DAS Audio (DAW).
3. Play something in your DAW — if the OBS audio meter moves, you're done.

Discord
-------

1. Confirm the Discord indicator in DAS Send shows a green "OK".
2. In Discord, open Share Screen.
3. To share your DAW only, choose your DAW application under Apps.
4. To include VST windows or other windows, choose your monitor under Screen.
5. Start sharing.

There are no extra modes or companion apps in DAS Send. If a full-screen share
picks up audio from other Windows apps, switching to the DAW application share
limits it to your DAW only.

Status display
--------------

Green "OK"
  Working — OBS is receiving audio.

Gray "WAIT"
  Add DAS Audio (DAW) as a source in OBS.

Yellow "VB-CABLE"
  For Discord, install VB-CABLE and restart Windows.
  OBS continues to work in the meantime.

Yellow "1 ONLY"
  Remove extra DAS Send instances — only one is allowed.

Red "!"
  Restart OBS first, then your DAW.

Gray, no text
  DAS Send is bypassed.

Avoiding double audio
---------------------

- Insert DAS Send on the master bus once only.
- In OBS, don't capture the same DAW audio through both DAS Audio and Desktop Audio.
- In Discord, start only one screen share at a time.
- Don't change Discord's microphone or Windows default audio output.

Uninstall
---------

Close your DAW and OBS, then remove DawAudioStreamer from Installed apps in
Windows Settings. ASIO settings, DAW projects, OBS scenes, and any third-party
virtual audio drivers are not removed. A restart is only needed if files were
in use at uninstall time.

For more detail, see README.md in the install folder.
